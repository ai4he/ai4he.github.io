About

Weber Whelan is a Computer Science student in the Clemson Honors College who completed the departmental honors thesis Fermi-LLM: An LLM Service for Performing Fermi-LAT Data Analysis (presented April 29, 2026), advised by Dr. Carlos Toxtli as part of the NASA-funded FERMI-LLM project. Weber began research as part of the Honors College's EUREKA! program in 2023, studying agricultural biotechnology with Dr. Hong Luo, and has been named to Clemson's President's List.
