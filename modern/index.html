Clemson University · Human-Centered Computing

Designing AI that expands what people can achieve.

The Human-AI Empowerment Lab studies how artificial intelligence, increasingly autonomous and agentic, can empower people at work, in science and in society. We build and evaluate human-centered AI systems with the people who will use them.

Research

Six thrusts, one question: how can AI empower people?

Our work spans human-centered automation, the future of work, AI for science, human-autonomy teaming, trustworthy AI and inclusive AI, each grounded in studies with real users and funded by federal sponsors.

Books

The ideas behind the lab, in two books

Lab director Carlos Toxtli-Hernández is the author of two books with CRC Press / Taylor & Francis that frame the lab's agenda: how AI can empower people over the long term, and how automation can be designed with people at its center.
