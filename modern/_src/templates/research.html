Research

How can AI empower people over the long term?

We study empowerment as the degree of autonomy and self-determination that people and communities have — and ask how AI systems can expand it rather than erode it. Our work combines system building, controlled experiments, field deployments and participatory design across six research thrusts.

01

Human-centered

People are involved at every step of the AI pipeline — from defining goals and producing data to evaluating and correcting systems.

02

Use-inspired

Projects start from concrete settings — crowd platforms, research labs, rescue teams, classrooms — where AI can provide measurable value.

03

Evaluated in the wild

We test our tools with real users and field experiments, and release code and benchmarks so others can build on them.

04

Accountable

We audit the limits of AI — calibration, fairness, ethical consistency — and design mitigation and oversight mechanisms.
