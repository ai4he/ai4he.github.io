About

Emmanuel Alexis Zarazúa Rubio was a visiting undergraduate researcher with the HAIE Lab in summer 2024, part of a team of students from SABES in Guanajuato, Mexico, that worked remotely with Cecilia Delgado Solorzano on an AI app for advanced bilingual speakers and co-authored AI for Becoming Fluent: Designing an App for Advanced Bilingual Speakers (IEEE CAI 2025).

IEEE CAI 2025

AI for Becoming Fluent: Designing an App for Advanced Bilingual Speakers

Cecilia Delgado Solorzano, Sarah Mendoza, Fernando Morales Flores, Jesús Martínez García, Néstor Emmanuel Briones Ramírez, Emmanuel Alexis Zarazúa Rubio, Ventura Rivera Robles, Asís Heriberto Rangel Ortiz, Carlos Toxtli-Hernández

DOI Website

This research explores the difficulties advanced second-language learners face in becoming fluent and the poten-tial of AI to enhance language learning apps. Through surveys, focus groups, and collaborative design sessions, we investigated learners' difficulties and practice habits. The participants created paper prototypes for an app targeting speaking and listening skills at an advanced level. Key findings suggest that fluency de-velopment requires exposure to diverse accents from both native and non-native speakers. The resulting design incorporates AI in the form of a chatbot with voice recognition capabilities. This study contributes to our understanding of advanced language learning needs and the potential of AI to address them, paving the way for innovative language learning solutions. Our findings highlight opportunities to develop more effective tools for learners striving to achieve fluency in their target languages.
